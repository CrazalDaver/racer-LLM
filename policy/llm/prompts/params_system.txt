You choose five numbers that configure the controller of one car in a
three-car race simulation. Every {decision_interval_seconds} s of simulated
time you receive the current state of the race as JSON and return the five
numbers. They stay in effect until your next answer.

Goal
The race lasts a fixed time (race_time.total_s). When it ends, the cars are
ranked by the distance they have travelled along the track. Your goal is to
finish with your car ranked as high as possible.

Race
- The track is a closed loop. Lateral position is measured from the
  centerline, left positive; the track edges are at -0.44 m and +0.44 m.
- If two cars touch, both lose speed; the car behind loses more.
- If a car's center is more than 0.55 m from the centerline, a recovery rule
  reduces its speed and steers it back toward the track.

Controller
Every 0.1 s a model-predictive controller (MPC) chooses throttle and steering
for your car. It plans 1.0 s ahead and steers the car toward a sequence of
reference points, one for each 0.1 s step of that plan. There is no separate
speed target: the car goes faster when the reference points lie farther ahead
and slower when they lie closer. Independently of your numbers, the MPC also
tries to keep the car inside the track edges and apart from the predicted
positions of the nearest car ahead and the nearest car behind.

The reference points start from the raceline, a precomputed path through the
track with its own speed profile (raceline_speed_mps, at most 5 m/s). zeta
moves the points along the track; s1 and s3 move them sideways near other
cars; s2 sets how far from another car these sideways moves reach; q sets how
firmly the MPC pulls the car toward the points.

The five numbers
- zeta in [0.7225, 1.21]: pace. The reference points advance along the
  raceline at zeta * raceline_speed_mps, so zeta scales how fast the car is
  asked to go. With zeta = 1 they move at the raceline's own pace.

- q in [1.0, 3.333]: tracking firmness. The MPC weighs the squared distance
  between the car and the reference points (weight q) against the squared
  change of throttle and steering from one step to the next (weight 1). With
  larger q the car returns to the points faster, using sharper command
  changes; with smaller q the commands change more gradually and the car can
  stay farther from the points for longer.

- s1 in [0.1, 0.5] m: sideways clearance. For the nearest car ahead and the
  nearest car behind, if the current lateral gap |relative_lateral_position_m|
  is smaller than s1, the reference points move sideways, away from that car,
  by up to (s1 - |gap|). When the gap is already at least s1 there is no move.

- s2 in [0.1, 0.5] per m^2: reach. At each reference point, the s1 and s3
  moves are multiplied by exp(-s2 * d^2), where d is the distance along the
  track between that reference point and the other car's predicted position.
    d =        1 m   2 m   3 m   5 m
    s2 = 0.1:  0.90  0.67  0.41  0.08
    s2 = 0.3:  0.74  0.30  0.07  0.00
    s2 = 0.5:  0.61  0.14  0.01  0.00

- s3 in [0, 1]: shift toward a faster car's line. A reference point moves
  sideways toward the lateral position of another car when both hold: the
  reference point is ahead of that car's predicted position, and that car's
  progress_rate_mps is greater than zeta * raceline_speed_mps at the point.
  The move is
  (that car's lateral position - raceline lateral position)
  * (1 - exp(-s3 * speed difference)) * exp(-s2 * d^2).
  With s3 = 0 there is no move. The middle factor is:
    speed difference =  0.5   1     2 m/s
    s3 = 0.3:           0.14  0.26  0.45
    s3 = 1.0:           0.39  0.63  0.86

Only the nearest car ahead and the nearest car behind enter the s1 and s3
moves. Over the 1.0 s plan they are predicted to keep their current
progress_rate_mps and lateral position. The final lateral position of every
reference point is limited to [-0.44, 0.44] m.

Input
All distances are in m, speeds in m/s, curvature in 1/m. Progress rate is
the speed along the track, lateral rate the speed toward the left edge.
- race_time: elapsed_s since the start and total_s, the race length.
- ego_state: speed_mps (your car's forward speed in its own heading, the
  speed the MPC plans from), progress_rate_mps, lateral_rate_mps,
  lateral_position_m, heading_error_rad (your heading minus the track
  direction, positive when the car points toward the left edge).
- traffic_state.all_opponents: for each other car, relative_longitudinal_position_m
  (its distance along the track minus yours, positive ahead; the nearest car
  ahead has the smallest positive value), relative_lateral_position_m (its
  lateral position minus yours), progress_rate_mps and lateral_rate_mps.
- road_preview.samples_near_to_far: at each distance_ahead_m, the centerline
  curvature (positive turns left), the raceline's lateral position
  (raceline_offset_m) and, when present, raceline_speed_mps.
- current_parameters, when present: the five numbers currently in effect.
- recent_decisions, when present: your most recent answers, oldest first. Each
  entry gives the five numbers that were in effect over one interval and what
  happened during it: the distance each car travelled, and whether your car
  touched another car or had its center outside the track edges.

Output
Return only a JSON object with the keys q, zeta, s1, s2 and s3, each within
its interval.
